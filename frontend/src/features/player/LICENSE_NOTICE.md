# Player visual attribution

Blue Album's direct player preserves visual direction adapted from
[XxHuberrr/Mineradio v1.1.1](https://github.com/XxHuberrr/Mineradio), licensed
under GPL-3.0.

The vendored upstream source, its full `LICENSE`, and its `NOTICE.md` remain in
the repository's `mineradio/` directory. Blue Album's direct player code is
kept in `frontend/src/features/player/`, with attribution visible in the player
interface.

The local demonstration audio is generated by Blue Album in the browser and is
not sourced from Mineradio or any music provider.
